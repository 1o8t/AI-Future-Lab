# Contributing

AI Future Lab is currently an early concept and architecture project.

Constructive feedback is welcome in areas such as:

- Urban planning
- Smart cities
- Artificial intelligence
- Digital twins
- Geospatial systems
- Sustainability
- Explainable AI
- Product design
- Responsible technology

Please keep feedback respectful, specific, and educational.

Do not submit private personal data, confidential datasets, credentials, or legally restricted information.
