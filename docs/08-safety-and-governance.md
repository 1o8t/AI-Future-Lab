# 08 — Safety and Governance

## Human Responsibility

AI Future Lab is a decision-support concept. Final decisions must remain with qualified humans and responsible authorities.

## Required Safeguards

- Clear distinction between generated concepts and approved plans
- Human review before important decisions
- Documentation of assumptions
- Visibility of uncertainty
- Version history
- Audit trail
- Role-based access
- Data minimization
- Protected private information
- Rollback to stable versions
- Incident response

## High-Risk Areas

The system must be especially careful when recommendations affect:

- Public safety
- Emergency access
- Housing access
- Transportation equity
- Environmental protection
- Critical infrastructure
- Protected land
- Legal compliance
- Personal or population data

## Fairness

Optimization should not benefit one group while silently creating harm for another.

Plans should be reviewed for:

- Access to services
- Travel burden
- Housing variety
- Accessibility
- Environmental exposure
- Public-space distribution
- Infrastructure reliability

## Explainability

Every major recommendation should include:

- What was recommended
- Why it was recommended
- Which data and assumptions were used
- What alternatives were considered
- What trade-offs exist
- What uncertainty remains
- Who must approve the decision

## Learning Controls

The system should not automatically adopt every user outcome.

Improvement should follow:

```text
Feedback
  ↓
Analysis
  ↓
Sandbox Test
  ↓
Safety Validation
  ↓
Human Approval
  ↓
Limited Release
  ↓
Monitoring
  ↓
Approval or Rollback
```
