## Workflow Reading Guide

- Blue: AI and system processes
- Mint/Green: execution
- Orange: decisions
- Yellow: review and retry
- Red: errors and recovery
- Pink/Purple: AI engine, runtime, or experience
- Gray: approved states, milestones, and notes
