# 01 — Project Vision

## Mission

To help people transform ideas for future cities into structured, testable, explainable, and responsible urban plans.

## Vision Statement

AI Future Lab aims to become an intelligent planning environment where human creativity and professional urban knowledge are supported by artificial intelligence.

The system should allow a user to communicate naturally, while the AI handles the complexity of organizing requirements, evaluating trade-offs, constructing planning scenarios, and explaining results.

## Core Objective

The project is designed to answer a central question:

> How can artificial intelligence help humans explore and improve city-planning ideas before expensive real-world decisions are made?

## Long-Term Direction

A mature AI Future Lab could support:

- Early-stage master planning
- Smart-city strategy
- Development scenario testing
- Climate-resilience planning
- Infrastructure coordination
- Public consultation
- Education and research
- Real-estate development analysis
- Municipal decision support

## Non-Goals

AI Future Lab should not:

- Replace licensed planners, architects, engineers, or authorities
- Present generated concepts as legally approved plans
- Hide uncertainty or assumptions
- Make irreversible decisions without human approval
- Use private data without clear authorization
- Optimize only for one metric while ignoring social impact

## Success Definition

The concept succeeds when it can clearly demonstrate:

- A strong connection between human intent and generated plans
- Transparent assumptions
- Measurable planning outcomes
- Useful scenario comparisons
- Explainable recommendations
- Safe human control
